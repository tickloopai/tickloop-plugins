# Handbook

For the people of a team or company: who they are, how they work together and the rules they follow. Usually shared with the team only, not public.

**The template starts with** a sample handbook for a fictional team: Categories `about-us` (About us), `how-we-work` (How we work), `onboarding` (Joining the team) and `policies` (Policies), and the entries `mission`, `values`, `communication`, `meetings`, `documentation`, `first-week`, `time-off`, `expenses` and `equipment`. Replace the samples with the user's entries (see "Sample entries" in the Skill); never carry over a sample policy's figures.

## Plan

- **About us**: mission and values, history, who does what (teams and roles, not a list of every person).
- **How we work**: communication, meetings, decisions, planning, tools, onboarding for newcomers.
- **Policies**: time off, expenses, equipment, security, conduct; each one entry.
- Add Categories the team needs ("Engineering", "Sales") rather than one long list.

## Entries

- Policies state exactly what applies: who, how much, how to ask, who approves, by when. Take every rule, amount and deadline from what the user gives you; a policy you are unsure of is a question for the user, never a guess.
- How-we-work entries describe what the team does now, with the tools and channels named as they are.
- Write for a newcomer on their first day: no internal shorthand without saying what it means.
- Name no private details about individuals (salaries, health, home addresses).

## Example

```markdown
# Time off

Everyone gets 25 days of paid time off a year, plus public holidays where they live.

1. Ask in **Time off** at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.
2. Your manager approves or answers within two working days.

Sick days do not count toward the 25; tell your manager the same morning.
```
