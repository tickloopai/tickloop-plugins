# Help center

For people who use a product and need an answer now: how to do something, why something behaves as it does, what to do when it breaks.

**The template starts with** a sample help center for a fictional notes app, Acme Notes: Categories `getting-started` (Getting started), `account` (Account and plans) and `troubleshooting` (Troubleshooting), and the entries `welcome`, `quick-start`, `install`, `sign-in`, `reset-password`, `plans`, `sync-issues`, `common-problems` and `contact-us`. Replace the samples with the user's entries (see "Sample entries" in the Skill); rename or replace the Categories once you know the product.

## Plan

1. List what readers come to do, from the user's product and its sources: sign up and sign in, the core tasks, settings, billing, account and data, and the problems they meet. Each becomes one entry.
2. Group them into 4 to 8 Categories named by task ("Get started", "Work with projects", "Billing and plans", "Account and security", "Fix a problem"). Subcategories only inside a Category of more than about eight entries.
3. Order: Get started first, problems last; inside a Category, the most asked first.

## Entries

- Mostly **how-to** entries, one task each, steps naming the interface exactly as it reads in that language.
- A **concept** entry for each idea readers must understand to use the product (plans, limits, roles, sharing), linked from the how-tos that need it.
- **Troubleshooting** entries titled by the symptom, and one entry on how to reach a person when nothing helps.
- An **FAQ** only for short questions that fit nowhere else.
- `welcome`: what the product is in two sentences and the three things to do first, each linking its entry by slug.

## Example

```markdown
# Invite a teammate

Invite someone to your workspace so they can open and edit its projects.

You need to be the workspace's owner or an admin.

1. Open **Settings** › **Members**.
2. Choose **Invite** and enter their email address.
3. Choose **Send invite**.

They appear under **Pending** until they accept. If they get no email, see [An invite email doesn't arrive](invite-email-missing).
```
